The purchase is made here on the site, and the subscription activates in the app itself — right after the payment. Prices come from the server; payment opens the secure YooKassa checkout page (card, SBP, SberPay, YuMoney).

Enter the payment code from the app

Open Vault → Settings → Privacy → Pro plan → tap “Copy code”. It is your account fingerprint, not a password: it cannot be used to sign in — only to attach a plan you have already paid for.
