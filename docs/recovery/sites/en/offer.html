2. Definitions

  • App — the Vault program for Android and desktop systems, distributed free of charge.
  • Relay — the Provider's server that accepts an encrypted envelope (ciphertext) and delivers it to the recipient faster.
  • Plan — a set of limits and relay access conditions (Free, Pro, Business).
  • Subscription — the right to use a paid plan for the paid period.

3. Subject

3.1. The Provider grants the User paid access to extended limits and priority delivery through the relay for the subscription period, and the User pays for that access.

3.2. The app, encryption, messaging through the Users' mail servers and other free features are provided free of charge and are not the subject of this offer. Payment does not grant access to message contents, encryption keys or message history.

3.3. The service is provided remotely through the app; no physical delivery, pickup or personal presence is required.

6. Provision of access

No shipping required. The service is provided remotely: plan access is activated in the app automatically as soon as payment is confirmed by the payment service. There is no physical delivery or pickup.

6.1. The service is provided within 15 minutes after payment confirmation. Access is normally activated within seconds.

6.2. If activation takes longer than 15 minutes and the User contacts support, the subscription is extended by the delay period.

7. Term and renewal

7.1. The subscription is valid for the selected period — 1, 3, 6, 9 or 12 months — from activation.

7.2. There is no automatic renewal or automatic charging. When the term ends, the plan reverts to free limits; data and message history are retained.

7.3. If an extension is paid before the current term ends, the new term is added to the remaining period.

8. Refunds

8.1. If the service was not provided (access not activated, duplicate payment, relay unavailable due to the Provider's fault), the amount paid is refunded in full.

8.2. If a subscription was paid but not used, the User may cancel and receive a full refund within 14 calendar days of payment. For later cancellations, the cost of the unused period is refunded upon request.

8.3. A refund request is sent to vault-msg@ya.ru and must include the payment date and amount and the User's contact details.

8.4. Refunds are made to the original payment method within 10 business days of receiving the request.

9.2. The User undertakes to use the services lawfully, not to disrupt the relay and not to interfere with the service.

9.3. The User is responsible for their own mailbox and for the safety of passwords and encryption keys.

10.2. The Provider cannot read message contents: the relay processes ciphertext only.

10.3. The Provider's liability is limited to the amount actually paid by the User for the relevant subscription period.
