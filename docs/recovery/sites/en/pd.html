2. Categories of personal data

  • user's email address (also the sign-in identifier — there is no separate account);
  • name or nickname the user chooses for the profile;
  • email addresses of contacts the user adds;
  • technical pseudonymous delivery identifier (derived from the public key fingerprint);
  • IP address (hashed) and technical request counters;
  • device push token;
  • payment details: payment ID, amount, date, plan, status;
  • location — only if the user sends an SOS message (coordinates are inserted by the device; the service does not receive them);
  • support requests: email address and message content.

Not processed: special categories of personal data (race, health, political views, etc.), biometrics, criminal record data; conversation content and encryption keys (they remain on the device).

3. Purposes

  • delivering encrypted messages between users (the core service);
  • operating the delivery service: envelope routing, daily quotas, spam and overload protection;
  • push notifications about new events (without message content);
  • accepting and recording subscription payments, issuing receipts (self-employed tax regime);
  • replying to user requests.

There is no processing for advertising, marketing profiling or analytics; the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Legal bases

  • Art. 6(1)(5) of Law No. 152-FZ — processing is necessary for a contract to which the data subject is a party (public offer);
  • Art. 6(1)(1) — consent for optional features (SOS with coordinates, support, optional notifications);
  • Art. 6(1)(2) and Law No. 422-FZ — compliance with tax accounting obligations.

5. How data is processed

Mixed processing: with and without automation. Operations: collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, rectification, retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access), blocking, deletion, destruction.

Security measures: TLS in transit; end-to-end encryption of content; keys only on the device; restricted server access; encrypted envelopes deleted within 24 hours; secrets kept outside the repository; in case of an incident — notification to Roskomnadzor within the statutory period (24 hours) and notification of affected users.

6. Third parties and cross-border transfer

  • the user's email provider — processes messages as ordinary email;
  • the payment provider — processes the payment and issues the receipt;
  • the push notification provider — Google LLC (USA), Firebase Cloud Messaging: receives the technical signal and pseudonymous device token, without message content and without the email address;
  • authorised bodies — only upon a lawful request.

Data is never sold or shared for advertising.

Cross-border transfer (Art. 12 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ). The only recipient outside Russia is Google LLC (USA), Firebase Cloud Messaging. Data involved: the device push token and a technical “new event” signal; the email address, name and message content are not transferred. Purpose: delivering a notification while the app is closed. Legal bases: performance of the contract with the data subject (Art. 6(1)(5)) and the subject's consent; the transfer is declared in the personal data processing notification filed with Roskomnadzor. Disabling push in the app or system stops the transfer.

8. Data subject rights and deletion

A user may request information about processing, demand rectification, blocking or destruction, and withdraw consent. Requests: vault-msg@ya.ru from the address concerned.

  • local data is deleted in the app (Settings → Privacy/Data) or together with the app;
  • server-side technical records (pseudonymous identifier, push token) are deleted on request within 30 days;
  • encrypted envelopes are deleted automatically within 24 hours;
  • payment records are retained by law and are not deleted early.

9. Information for app stores

For verification of personal data requirements (e.g. section 5.2 of the RuStore moderation rules):

  • the data controller is identified and the responsible person is appointed (section 1);
  • the privacy policy is available from the store listing and inside the app (Settings → Privacy) — /privacy.html;
  • data categories and purposes are disclosed (sections 2–3);
  • permissions (camera, microphone, location, notifications, files) are justified by functionality and requested when used (section 8 of the policy);
  • legal bases are stated (section 4);
  • localisation is observed: the storage server is in Russia (section 6);
  • cross-border transfer is disclosed: recipient Google LLC (USA) — push token only, no email address or content (section 6);
  • the deletion procedure is described and available in the app (section 8);
  • there is no hidden profiling and no advertising/analytics SDKs (section 3).
