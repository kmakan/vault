Privacy Policy

Revision dated 10 November 2026. Applies to the Vault application (Android, desktop) and the vault-msg.tech website.

In short. Vault does not store your conversations on its servers: messages are encrypted on your device, keys never leave it, and we hold no message database. The app does process a minimal set of data without which delivery is technically impossible — your email address, technical delivery identifiers and payment details. Below we state exactly what that data is, why it is needed, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

1. Who is the data controller

The data controller is Maksim A. Kozlov, a natural person applying the special tax regime “Professional Income Tax” (self-employed), INN 774338097699.

The person responsible for organising personal data processing is the same person. Contact for any questions or requests: vault-msg@ya.ru.

Controller details and processing information are summarised on a separate page: Personal Data Processing.

2. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract with the user (Art. 6(1)(5) of Federal Law No. 152-FZ): without the email address and technical delivery identifiers, message delivery is impossible. The contract is the public offer published on this site.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(1)) — for optional features: sending an SOS message with coordinates, contacting support, optional notifications.
  • Compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(2)) — for payment records and tax accounting (Federal Law No. 422-FZ).

3.3 Subscription payment data

Paid plans are sold on the website through a payment provider (YuKassa; for payments from outside Russia — a crypto provider). We receive and store: payment ID, amount, date, plan and status. We never receive or store card details — they are handled by the payment provider.

Payment records are kept for the period required by tax law (4 years). Receipts are issued via the “My Tax” app.

3.4 Support requests

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of the message — solely to reply and resolve the issue. Kept until the request is closed and for a reasonable period afterwards.

4. Location, camera, microphone, files

This data is processed on the device and only when you use the feature yourself:

  • Location — only for the SOS message: coordinates are inserted into the text you send to a chosen recipient. We never receive or store them.
  • Microphone — voice messages and calls. The recording reaches the recipient encrypted.
  • Camera — QR code when adding a contact and video in calls.
  • Files and media — only those you choose to send.

5. What we never do

  • we do not read your conversations — we technically cannot: we hold no keys;
  • we do not build an address book database of our own, and never sell or share data with ad networks;
  • we use no advertising or analytics SDKs, build no profiles and do no hidden profiling;
  • we do not ask for a phone number and require no separate registration in our database;
  • we do not transfer data to third parties except as listed in section 7.

6. Notifications and cross-border transfer

Local notifications are generated on the device and send nothing out. For push notifications on Android we use Google FCM: only a technical “new event” signal and a pseudonymous device token are transferred. Message content never passes through the push service. This is the only transfer outside Russia; the delivery server is located in Russia.

Cross-border transfer. Recipient: Google LLC (USA), Firebase Cloud Messaging. What is transferred: the device push token and a technical “new event” signal — no email address, no name, no message content. Purpose: deliver a notification while the app is closed. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(5) of Federal Law No. 152-FZ) and your consent, given together with notification permission; the transfer is declared in the personal data processing notification under Art. 12 of the same law. The cross-border transfer notification was registered in the Roskomnadzor information system on 18.11.2026 (number 100494565). You can disable push in the app or system settings — the transfer then stops.

7. Third parties

  • Your email provider — processes messages as ordinary email under its own policy. You choose the provider.
  • Payment provider — receives the data required to process the payment and issue a receipt.
  • Push notification provider — Google LLC (USA), FCM service: receives the technical signal and pseudonymous device token, without message content and without the email address (section 6).
  • Legal requirements — upon a lawful request from an authorised body. Conversation content and keys do not exist on our side and cannot be provided.

9. Your rights

You may request information about processing of your data, demand its rectification, blocking or destruction, and withdraw consent. Write to vault-msg@ya.ru from the address concerned; we reply within 10 business days (the period may be extended with notice).

10. Data deletion

  • In the app: Settings → Privacy/Data — delete history, contacts, keys and the local database; uninstalling the app removes local data entirely.
  • On the delivery server: the pseudonymous identifier, push token and related technical records are deleted upon request to vault-msg@ya.ru within 30 days. Encrypted envelopes are deleted automatically within 24 hours.
  • Payment records are kept for the period required by tax law and cannot be deleted earlier.

Important: encryption keys are stored only on your device. If you uninstall the app without a backup and without your recovery key, restoring access to old conversations will be impossible — a consequence of no one but you holding the keys.

11. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data about them; if such data reaches us by mistake, we delete it upon request.

12. Changes to this policy

The current revision is always published at vault-msg.tech/privacy.html. For material changes we update the revision date and, where the legal bases of processing are affected, notify users in the app.
